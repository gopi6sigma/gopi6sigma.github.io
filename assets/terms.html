Registration Terms And Conditions

  • Exam Date and Time: The exam is held in two rounds, both on Sundays at 11:00 AM. Preliminary Exam: December 13th, 2026. Main Exam: December 20th, 2026 (for candidates who qualify in the Preliminary Exam).
  • Reporting Time: Students must arrive at the examination venue one hour before the exam starts, i.e. by 10:00 AM. Entry to the exam hall is allowed until 10:50 AM; no one will be allowed to enter after 10:50 AM.
  • Hall Ticket Requirement: Each candidate must present either a printed or electronic copy of their Hall Ticket to be admitted to the exam. Failure to do so will result in the candidate being unable to write the exam.
  • Aadhaar Card: Candidates are required to bring their Aadhaar card as a valid form of identification for the exam.
  • Mobile Devices: The use of mobile phones or any electronic communication devices is strictly prohibited within the examination hall.
  • Transportation: No transportation services will be provided to candidates. Students are responsible for arranging their own transportation to the exam venue at their own expense.
  • Exam Center and Details: Once registered, candidates are not allowed to change their assigned exam center or any other registration details. Please review your registration information carefully before submission.
  • Upload recent photo only (if you are not using webcam). Registrations with old photos uploaded are considered invalid.
  • Verification Standards: Sadisha reserves the right to disqualify any registration that does not meet our verification standards. Ensure that all provided information is accurate and in compliance with the registration requirements.
  • Disclaimer Regarding College Term Challenges: Sadisha does not hold responsibility for any challenges or issues you may encounter throughout your college term.

Please read and understand these terms and conditions carefully before proceeding with your exam registration. Compliance with these guidelines is essential to ensure a smooth and fair examination process.
